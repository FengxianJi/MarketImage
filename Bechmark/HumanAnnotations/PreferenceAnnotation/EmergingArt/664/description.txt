Title: Nuremberg Chronicle, Folio CI - View of Metis (Metz-France) - 1493

Artist: Michael Wohlgemuth (1434-1519)
Year: 1493
Technique: Woodcut print
Dimensions: 4.6x3.2x0.01 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Imaginary view of Metz. 
Several small woodcuts on the backside showing famous people from the time. 
Very early incunabula sheet with several very nice woodcuts, from the 15th century. 
As early as one can go. Sheet with several woodcuts from the first edition of Hartmann Schedel, "Liber Cronicarum", printed by Anton Koberger in Nuremberg on 12 July 1493. 
Dimensions: 
Sheet: 46.00 x 32.00 cm 
Metz woodcut: woodcut:  19.20 x 22.60 cm 
Literature: 
Liber Chronicarum, here a first edition , printed by Anton Koberger in Nuremberg on 12 July 1493. 
The 'Schedelsche Weltchronik' was the earliest and most richly illustrated incunabula. The literature indicates Albrecht Dürer spent his apprenticeship years in Koberger's workshop. Many woodcuts that appeared in the world chronicle show the style of Albrecht Dürer. 
The woodcuts were made in the workshop of Michael Wolgemut, the well-known teacher of Albrecht Dürer, and his stepson Wilhelm Pleydenwurff.  Wohlgemut was the tutor of Albrecht Dürer between 1486-90.

Very good considering it's age, a nice clean sheet. 
The outer margins with occasional tear.
