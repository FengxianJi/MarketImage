Title: Vu pris a Londres

Artist: Giuseppe De Nittis
Year: 1876
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 140×0.1×217 mm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Giuseppe De Nittis (Barletta 1846 - Saint Germain en Laye 1884)
VUE PRIS À LONDRES
Etching and drypoint. 140x217 mm. Sheet: 243x315 mm.
One of the artist's most famous engravings. Reworking in counterpart of Sotto il viadotto a painting executed in London between 1876 and 1880. Exemplary in the second state out of 3 before publication in the "Gazette des Beaux-Arts" of November 1884 and therefore forward letter. Excellent impression printed with strong and marked sign and slight plate-tone on laid paper. In the lower right corner dry stamp of the Prandi Library of Reggio Emilia. Large margins, traces of oxidation from an old passe-partout, excellent conservation.
