Title: Juno grabbing Callisto who's changing in a bear - First state

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617)
Year: 1590
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 175×250×1 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
By Hendrick Goltzius (workshop of)

Juno angrily grabbing Callisto's hair who lies on the ground at centre and who slowly changed into a bear, Juno's chariot in left background; Engraving

Four lines of Latin in two columns by Estius:"Magna Jouis coniux ... / ... ut ursa foret."
First state ( Before the second number at right)

Reference:

A good impression on early watermarked laid paper trimmed on the image borderline.
Tipped on a collection sheet with top corners.
17,50 x 25 cm on plate border.

An early work drawn by Goltzius en engraved in his workshop. Print from the second series of scenes from the Metamorphoses whose design occupied Goltzius about 1589-1590. The design sketches for this prints are recorded in some of the greatest museums.

Goltzius made the drawings as designs for the prints. He probably also surveyed the engraving process by a member of his workshop and was involved in the publishing of the series. The mannerist style is at it's best here.
