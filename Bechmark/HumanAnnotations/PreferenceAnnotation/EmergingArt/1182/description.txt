Artist: Marc Chagall (1887-1985)
Year: 1957
Technique: Lithograph
Dimensions: 24×18×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Marc Chagall (1887-1985) - “Self-portrait to scale” 
Lithograph on light vellum - 1957 
Lithograph specially executed by Chagall in 1957, who set it on stone in the Mourlot workshops. 
This is the original print and not a later reproduction. 
A pretty soft green self-portrait of the painter, in profile, behind which a blue ladder seems to rise to the sky. As Chagall worked on this engraving to illustrate a monograph on his life, the long ladder probably symbolizes his life path. 
Catalog raisonné Sauret/Mourlot: Vol. II, p. 25, N° 200 (see photo of the catalog raisonné)
