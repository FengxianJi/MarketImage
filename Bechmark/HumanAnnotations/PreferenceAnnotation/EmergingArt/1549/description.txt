Title: The Holy Family with the infant St John

Artist: Simone Cantarini ( 1612-1648 )
Year: 1640
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 145×103×1 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
The Holy Family with the infant St John; St Joseph stands in the centre behind
Etching after Cantarini.

By an engraver who worked near to the original work.
The British Museum holds the same print in her collection (N° 1876,0212.22)

Reference:
Bartsch / Le Peintre graveur (XIX.131.15)
Massari 1997 / In 'Simone Cantarini detto il Pesarese', ed. Andrea Emiliani (5)

130 x 90 mm
Good impression on laid paper with full plate border and mostly tread margins.
Remains of album tipping at the corners of the backside.

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
