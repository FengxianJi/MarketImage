Title: Folio engraving, inner view of the Quirinal Palace, Rome, Italy

Artist: Joachim I Von Sandrart (1606-1688)
Year: 1675
Technique: Engraving, Watercolour
Dimensions: 36.5×23.6×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Architectural facade of the building, taken from the monumental Academia Todesca della Architectura, Scultura & Pittura: oder Teutsche Academie der Edlen Bau, Bild-und Mahlerey-Künste (Deutsche Academy of Architecture - Sculpture – Painting). 
The first edition, in German, was published by Joachim von Sandrart in Nuremberg, in three volumes, respectively in 1675, 1679 and 1680. 
Sandrart's works are inspired by the series Palazzi di Roma by Pietro Ferrerio. This series of etchings is divided into two books showing the plans and architecture of various buildings in Rome. 
Etching, with wide margins, very good condition. Recent hand colour.
