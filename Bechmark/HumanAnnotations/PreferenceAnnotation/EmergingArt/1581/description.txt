Title: Esto es peor

Artist: Francisco De Goya y Lucientes (1746-1828)
Year: 1810
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: N/A
Condition: Overall in good condition with traces of ageing and usage

Description:
Beautiful etching on laid paper. With wide margins.

Plate 37 of the series "Los Desastres de La Guerra".

Goya was born in Fuendetodos (near Saragossa, Aragon) 30 March 1746 and died Bordeaux 16 April 1828; one of the most celebrated Spanish artists, known of by 1801 as the 'Apelles of Spain' and his great body of works includes some 700 paintings, 900 drawings and almost 300 prints and served three generations of Spanish monarchs; King Charles III and IV and Ferdinand VII. In his teens studied in Saragossa under José Luzán Martínez (1710-1785) for four years who had painted in Naples when it was under the rule of the future King Charles III of Spain).
