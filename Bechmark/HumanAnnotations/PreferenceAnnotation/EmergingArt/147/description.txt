Description:
"Lichballet V", an original silkscreen by German artist Otto Piene, circa 1972. 
Piene and Heinz Mack were the co-founders of the art movement ZERO that included artists such as Günther Uecker, Jean Tinguely, Piero Manzoni and Yves Klein.  These artists came together with the collective desire to move away from subjective post-war movements such as Art Informel and Tachisme. The main objective was to create art that was purely about the work’s materials and the world in which those materials exist, de-emphasising the role of the artist’s hand. The focus was on light and space.
