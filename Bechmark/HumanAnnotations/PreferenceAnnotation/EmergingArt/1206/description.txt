Title: The prodigal son seeking food by the pigs

Artist: Jacques Callot (1592-1635)
Year: 1635
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 61×80×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
A farmyard with, on the left, the prodigal son, in rags, talking to his master, who stands next to a building; herd of pigs in the middle; third state with plate number. 1635
Etching

With name of publisher, number and two lines of French verses: 'Celuy qui dédaignoit... destinez aux porceaux'.

Print made by: Jacques Callot (1592-1635)
Published by: Israël Henriet (1590-1661)
Callot's publisher. Son of the Nancy painter Claude Henriet (d.1605), and Callot's best friend.

Reference:
Lieure 1927 / Jacques Callot (1409.III)
Meaume 1860 / Recherches sur la vie et les ouvrages de Jacques Callot (58.III)

From a series of eleven plates relating the parable of the prodigal son.

6,10 x 8,00 cm.
Tipped with top border on a collection sheet. Remains of former album tipping on the backside of the corners.

By Jacques Callot ( 1592 - 1635)
Etcher and draughtsman, born Nancy. Working in Rome and Florence for the Medic; 1621 returned to Nancy, where he settled and married. Died suddenly in 1635. From c. 1620 most of his plates were published by his friend Israel Henriet.
