Title: De rattenvanger met zijn knecht

Artist: Cornelis II Visscher (1629-1658), naar
Year: 1660
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 38.5×32.3×1 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
"Fele fugas mures! (...)" 
A rat poison seller with his dog and servant. The boy is holding a staff with a rat cage. The seller carries a bag and tools with him, a rat walks on his cloak. 
Etching by an unknown maker. Copy in the same orientation as the original. After Cornelis II Visscher (1629-1658). 18th century. 
Catalog reference: Hollstein 50, blank copy. 
Extremely rare copy after this famous print by Visscher. Not in the collection of the Rijksmuseum Amsterdam (RPK). 
Good condition. Paper slightly browned, presumably due to an earlier framing. A repaired tear at the top to the image. Some light creases. Verso: blank, with remnants of an earlier confirmation.
