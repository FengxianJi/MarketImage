Title: Tableaux Vivants - P

Artist: Sonia Delaunay (1885-1979)
Year: 1969
Technique: Pochoir / Stencil
Dimensions: 28×19×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Pochoir (stencil) by Sonia Delaunay.

Published in 1969 by Edizioni del Naviglio, Milano, Italy. Printed by Jacomet on Vélin Aussedat paper.

Delaunay made 27 costume designs to illustrate the book "Tableaux Vivants" (Living Images). These were copied in pochoir/stencil technique popular at the time, and used to print this edition.

There is oxidation in one of the margins, which does not affect the image and would not be seen once framed.
