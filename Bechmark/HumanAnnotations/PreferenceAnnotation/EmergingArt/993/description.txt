Comment of the appraiser: In 1907, Robert Delaunay began his artistic work on the monuments of Paris, which he decided to continue in 1926 with the production of some twenty lithographs, including Paris, La Seine and the Tower. The influence of Cubism, and in particular Picasso, on the artist can be felt immediately in this lithograph. This lithograph is a characteristic work of the period when Delaunay questioned the relevance of cubism and futurism before moving on to abstraction with Orphism and Simultanism. To make the link and understand the work in more detail, we advise you to read the biography of the artist below.


Artist’s biography: In the early 20th century, the French-born artist Robert DELAUNAY (1885-1941) and his wife Sonia DELAUNAY-TERK were among the pioneers of abstraction along with KANDINSKY, MONDRIAN and MALEVITCH. Abstraction has its origins in the scientific understanding of visual and sound perception, but also in esotericism and spirituality. It will lead these artists to move away from the representation of reality towards a liberation of colour and line. For Robert DELAUNAY and the members of this artistic tendency, it is a personal, thoughtful, philosophical and emotional approach, enriched by travel, the surrounding political context but also by the Fauvist, Expressionist and Cubist movements which they assimilate while adding their experience. In the history of art, abstraction is not only a movement, it appears as a rupture between 1910 and 1920. Robert DELAUNAY is the main creator of two movements: Orphism and Simultanism.
Delaunay was part of an avant-garde generation that was particularly prolific artistically between 1912 and 1914. He had close ties (in correspondence, in art, even in friendship) with the poets Guillaume Apollinaire and Blaise Cendrars, the Russian painters Vassily Kandinsky and Michel Larionov, the German painters August Macke and Franz Marc, the Slovakian painter Geza Szobel.
After the war, he befriended the artists of the Surrealist movement, of whom he made several portraits, without adopting their points of view and their artistic visions. In particular, he had a strong and lasting friendship with the poet Tristan Tzara.
