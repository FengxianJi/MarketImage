Title: Without Bacchus and Ceres, Venus freezes - Rare

Artist: Crispijn de Passe (1564-1637)
Year: N/A
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 1.3x1.3x0.01 cm
Condition: Fair condition

Description:
This is an extremely rare engraving. It is never on the market and very hard to find info, if so the data are various and given to various artists, (De Bry, De Gheyn, De Passe) 
Fact is the border text and circular format is identical to the Goltzius print (New Hollstein 147 - Hollstein 305 - Hirschmann 305 - Strauss 1977 284 - Bartsch III.78.257) but with another central theme. 
While Goltzius shows a nude venus and Amor against a tree trunk, our artist shows a more decent dressed figures. We clearly recognize Ceres and Bacchus at left with Venus at right with the winged Amor next to her. 
This rare print is a good impression. It has some paper defects in the borders and is doubled on another sheet for re-enforcement. Time staining, mainly in the margins. 
Still a nice example of a very rare and  wanted subject.
