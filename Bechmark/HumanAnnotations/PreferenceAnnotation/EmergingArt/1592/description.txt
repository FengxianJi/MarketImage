Title: Lycaon Changed into a Wolf

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617)
Year: 1589
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 0.01×17.7×25.3 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Jupiter changes king Lycaon into a wolf; Metamorphoses from Ovid. Lycaon tried to cheat Jupiter by slaughtering his son Arcas and to offer him as a sacrament, on which Jupiter turns him into a wolf. In the background the king's palace is affected by lightning. In the margin a four-line caption, in two columns, in Latin.

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
