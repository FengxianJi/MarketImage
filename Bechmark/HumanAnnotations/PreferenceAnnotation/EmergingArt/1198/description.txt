Title: La Rivière Enchantée - Autoportrait avec Kimiyo

Artist: Tsuguharu (Léonard) Foujita (1886-1968)
Year: 1951
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: N/A
Condition: In very good condition

Description:
Tsuguharu (Léonard) Foujita (1886-1968
Isabey, my only friend (La femme au parfum)

Original etching
Signed in the plate
On vellum 52.5 x 38 cm

AUTHENTICITY:
This work appears in the catalog raisonné established by Sylvie Buisson under the reference 28.74 (volume II, page 506).

INFORMATION:
Foujita created this print for Isabey perfumes in 1928. It was produced in a small number of copies until the 1970s, for the brand's privileged customers.

Excellent condition

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
