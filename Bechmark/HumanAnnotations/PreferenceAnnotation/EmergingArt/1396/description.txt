This print is part of an artwork by Christopher Wool and Felix Gonzalez-Torres. Created originally in 1993 for the Printed Matter exhibition at Dia New York as a stack piece, it comprises an 8-inch stack of posters, specified by the artists as the "ideal height". Visitors to the exhibit could roll up a print and take it home with them. 
This 2019 re-edition was printed exclusively for an exhibition at the renowned Museum Abteiberg in Mönchengladbach, Germany. Just like at previous shows, visitors were allowed to take individual sheets off the top of the pile (see photos). This re-edition is licensed and reaches good results at urban & contemporary art auctions worldwide.
