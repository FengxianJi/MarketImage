Title: Resurrection of Christ - Centrepiece of the Last Judgement

Artist: Michelangelo (1475-1564), by Giorgio Mantovano Ghisi (c.1512/20-1582)
Year: 1540
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 50×63×0 cm
Condition: Fair condition

Description:
Beautiful, large etching on laid paper.

Plate D, from the 11 plates belonging to the Last judgement engraved by G. Ghisi, after Raphael frescoes in the Vatican.

Print made by: Giorgio Ghisi
After: Michelangelo
Published by: Giovanni Giacomo de' Rossi

Curator's comments (Source: British Museum)
The plates by Ghisi lettered A-L could be joined up like a jigsaw puzzle to form a large image of the whole Last Judgment, which Michelangelo had completed shortly before the prints were made. These were Ghisi's major project of the 1540s, and the most impressive of all the early prints made after the fresco.
