Title: Scena di battaglia

Artist: Jost Amman (1539-1591)
Year: 1570
Technique: Woodcut print
Dimensions: 1.46x1.61x0.0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Beautiful and rare plate from the series of eighty “Icones ducum Bavariae”, never officially published but survived in volumes of plates, which features full-length portraits of the Electors of Bavaria from 493 to 1583 with their coats of arms. 
Sheet trimmed along the frame line, on laid paper, preserved in excellent condition, visible signs of an old folding only on the reverse (see photos). 
Monogram "A.I." on the stone on the left. 
One of the dukes of Bavaria depicted in a military camp, full-length with hat and rich robes covering the armour, in his left hand the sceptre, at the side the sword, at the bottom the noble coat of arms, further back the tent with commanders who are examining a map, in the background the same duke on horseback and a castle. 
The New Hollstein: German engravings, etchings and woodcuts 1400-1700 (VIII.201.).
