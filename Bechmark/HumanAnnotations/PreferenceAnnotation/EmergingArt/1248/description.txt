A litograph from Toros y Toreros. Picasso was working on these litographs from 1957 to 1960.

A litograph from the Picasso's sketchbook on bull fighting. Second edition from 1962. 125 copies numbered, including 25 non-commercial copies and 1-5 copies include litographs enriched by the artist.

These drawings by Picasso covered a period from 1950 to 1960, during which time he enjoyed seeing Dominguin bullfight in the arenas of Nîmes, Arles, Frejus and Vallauris.

The leading copies of this publication contain a series of reproductions of color drawings selected from the first two notebooks, as well as a lithograph of February 7, 1961. (Wikipedia).
