Jos, Jobst or Joss. Painter, draughtsman, designer of woodcuts and etcher. 1539 Zurich. Died in Nuremberg in 1591 where he had settled in 1561, taking the place of Virgil Solis (d.1562). Became the chief designer for Sigmund Feierabend's publications in Frankfurt.
