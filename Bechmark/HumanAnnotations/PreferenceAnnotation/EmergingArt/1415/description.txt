Title: Prière

Artist: Kees van Dongen
Year: 1925
Technique: pochoir
Dimensions: 26.5×20.5×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
For the 1925 book about the oeuvre of Van Dongen, written by Ed. des Courieres had 8 pochoirs. These pochoirs are hand-colored lithographs.

This pochoir, called Prière. A woman who is praying.

This work is depicted and described on pages 136 and 137 of the book: Kees van Dongen, the complete graphic work. Written by Jan Juffermans and Jan Juffermans Jr.

The work is in excellent condition.
The protective sheet that is in the book in front is still there, completely authentic. If you want to frame the work, a framer can easily pick it up for you.

We ship flat and firmly packed between 2 wooden plates.
