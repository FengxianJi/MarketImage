Joan Miro - (1893 - 1983) - etching and aquatint - Engraved by Lacourière and Frélaut, published by Maeght Editeur, 1962

Framed with a mat, a silver baguette and a black frame - size of the frame : 37.5 x 30.5 cm
Dupin reference 74
Miro had used one of these subjects to make a greeting card for the Maeght Gallery in 1952 and reworked it for the deluxe edition of Yves Bonnefoy's book of poems "Anti-Platon" in 1962
