Title: Christ teaching in the temple. Christ among the doctors,Rare first state.

Artist: Giulio Clovio (1498-1578), Cornelis Cort (1533-1578)
Year: 1567
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 27.3×20.7×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Christ teaching in the temple; Christ sits enthroned with the elders ranged on either side.
Christ among the doctors.

After a painting of Giulio Clovio (1498-1578).
He was a famous painter specialized in Miniatures. He is the greatest illuminator of the Italian High Renaissance. He was a pupil of Giulio Romano.
Born in the kingdom of Croatia, he traveled from Hungary to Italy.
Giulio Clovio met Pieter Bruegel the Elder in Rome in 1553, and they worked together. In several miniatures of Giulio Clovio from this time is the hand of Pieter Bruegel the Elder.

Engraved by Cornelis Cort (1533-1578) worked in Antwerp, Venice, Florence and Rome.
Born in Edam, he started in Antwerp and went then to Italy. His engravings were published by Hiëronymus Cock in Antwerp and later in Rome.
Engravings after Giulio Clovio are very rare. Most of them engraved by Cornelis Cort, only 17 different by Cort in the British Museum.
