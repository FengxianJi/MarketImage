Description:
Marc Chagall (1887-1958) 
‘Le Bateau Mouche au bouquet et à la Tour Eiffel’. 
Lithograph set on stone by Chagall himself. 
Printed at Atelier Mourlot. 
Unsigned 
On Japan paper. 
Total size: 39 x 30 cm. 
Reference: Mourlo4t #352. 
This lithograph was created for the portfolio ‘Regards sur Paris’ published in 1962 by André Sauret and limited to 180 prints. This is probably a workshop proof print not included in the portfolio edition.
