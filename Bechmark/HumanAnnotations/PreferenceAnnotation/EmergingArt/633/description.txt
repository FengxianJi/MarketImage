Title: Diomedes kills Rhesus, king of Thrace. Odysseus steals the horses. Speculum Heroicum Homeri.

Artist: Crispijn I De Passe (1564-1637)
Year: 1604-1612
Technique: Engraving

Description:
Diomedes kills Rhesus, king of Thrace. Odysseus steals the horses from a tent camp. Superb dark early impression. 
Engraving nr 10 from the rare series of 24 engravings: Speculum Heroicum Homeri. 
Crispijn van de Passe (1589-1637). 
He was the first of the de Passe dynasty.
