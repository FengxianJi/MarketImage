Title: Caprichos Blatt 48: Soplones (Ohrenbläser)

Artist: Francisco De Goya (1746-1828)
Year: 1922
Technique: Etching, aquatint polished
Dimensions: 0×197×300 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
Francisco José De Goya y Lucientes (1746-1828)
Original copper print sheet no. 48 of the "Caprichos": "Soplones" (ear blowers)
Etching, aquatint polished

Hugo Schmidt publishing house, Munich, 1922
Edition of 500, here numbered engraving - copy # 10 (see photo)

Copper print in good condition
Sheet size: 300 x 197 mm
Image size: 185 x 129 mm

Explanation of the picture: Inquisition informants and the auricular confession as a perfidious source of amusement are represented here by the blowing demons with the attributes of malice, stupidity and lasciviousness.
