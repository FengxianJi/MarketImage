Title: Handcolored copperengravings of Suecia Antiqua et Hodierna

Artist: Van Padbrugge e.a. (XVII)
Year: N/A
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 30.0x30.25x0.25 cm
Condition: Fair condition

Description:
A collection of seven handcolored brass engravings depicting castle's, landscapes and churches in Sweden. These engravings were collected by the Swedish publisher Erik Dahlberg in the second half of the 17th century when Sweden was at the zenith of its power and wanted to be recognized in Europe as an Imperial power. They became part of the famous Atlas "Swedica Antiqua et Hodierna" ( Ancient and Modern Sweden) which was published between 1693-1714 and which was illustrated with 353 engravings. This set compromises: 
1) Templum St Catherina Holmia. 2) Arx Moorby. 3) Almerstaak and Arnoo. 4) Bogesundh and Burnsholm. 5) Ekeby Holm and Ekeby Hoff 6) St Birgit Ubsalam. 7) Carlsson 1662. Fantastic quality of coloring. With margins 38 x 30,5 cm. Provenance: Private collection Johannesburg.
