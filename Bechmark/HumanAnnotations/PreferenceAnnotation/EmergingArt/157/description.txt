Title: Vocalists accompanied by theorbo and shepherds horn

Artist: Francesco Bartolozzi (1727-1815), after Guercino (1591-1666)
Year: 1764
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 26.0x37.0x0.0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Beautiful etching on thick wove paper in brown ink. 
Curator's comments 
One of a number of prints engraved after drawings by Guercino and other masters and a Lady in the Royal Collection, chiefly by Bartolozzi. They were re-published in folio by Boydell in the 1790s. This appeared in Boydells' 'Eighty-two prints, engraved by F. Bartolozzi & c. from the original drawings of Guercino, in the collection of his Majesty'. 
(Source: British Museum) 
Condition is good. Some surface dirt. Diffuse foxing. Yellowing paper. Water stain.
