Title: Christmas: The adoration of the magi.

Artist: Johann Wilhelm Bauer (1607-1640), Melchior Küssel (1626-1684)
Year: 1670-1682
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 15.4×20.8×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Christmas: The adoration of the magi. The Christmas star shining above the stable with the holy family. 
After a drawing/painting of Johann Wilhelm Bauer (1607-1640). He was an Austrian painter, specialized in historical scenes. 
Etching by  Melchior Küssel (1626-1684). He was an etcher and engraver who worked in Augsburg. 
He was a pupil of Mathias Merian. Here he learned the high skills of engraving and etching. 
Published  in Augsburg ca 1670-1682. 
References: Hollstein German 342, 5th engraving of an album. 
In perfect condition. Good dark/grey impression with all the tiny details. 
Printed on thin laid paper with very large margins. 
With a very good watermark. 
Delivered in a museum passepartout.
