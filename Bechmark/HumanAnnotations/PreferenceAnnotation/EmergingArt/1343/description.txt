Title: "Giganti scalano il Monte Olimpo"

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617)
Year: 1589
Technique: Burin
Dimensions: 180×255×0 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
Beautiful copy trimmed within the beat, on laid paper with watermark, in very good condition, only a small gap compensated along the right edge (see photos).
Plate 7, number bottom left, from the series of 52 from "Ovid's Metamorphoses", one of the 40 invented by Hendrick Goltzius engraved in his workshop between 1589 and 1590.
Below the image Estius Latin inscription of two lines on two columns "Stat Briareus celum affectans ... / ... arma furens."
Depict Giants climbing Mount Olympus carrying large blocks of stone representing the mountains on Earth, in the center Jupiter seen from behind and standing trying to stop them, other gods fighting the giants from above the clouds.
New Hollstein (Dutch & Flemish), 532-551; Bartsch, III.104.7; Hollstein, 10-61 and 508-559.

Category: Art - Prints
