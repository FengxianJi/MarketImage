Shrigley is a painter, sculptor, filmmaker and photographer, and has recorded spoken word albums of his writing and poetry. He belongs with Damien Hirst, Tracey Emin, David Hockney, Banksy, Francis Bacon, Jenny Saville to the most important British contemporary artists.  This is an early screen print by David Shrigley in his  immediately recognisable style, characterised by crude line drawings, bold colours and offhand lettering.
