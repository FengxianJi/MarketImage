Pablo Picasso. 1881-1973 (after) Toros y Toreros 
Taken from the 1st sketchbook (after) by Pablo Picasso on Corrida. 
Lithograph from the suite "Toros y Toreros", published by the Cercle d'Art - Paris in 1961. 
Pablo Picasso worked on this project from 1957 to 1960. 
This lithograph is from the 1st edition published in 1961 and limited to 150 copies (including 125 numbered copies and 25 out of commerce) dated in the plate, printed on thick Arches paper by the Fernand Mourlot workshops, Paris
