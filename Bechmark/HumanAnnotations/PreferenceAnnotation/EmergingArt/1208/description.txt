Title: Salmon fishing

Artist: Hans Bol (1534-1593)
Year: 1580
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 74×217×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Venationis, piscationis, et aucupii typi (Hunting, Fishing and Fowling Scenes)

A group of fishermen create a river or inlet from the sea using fences and cone-shaped baskets (weals) to bring salmon from the open sea; at centre, fishermen hoist large fish onto a boat where a harpoon is visible; at right, a weal with a large fish in it is pulled onto shore; at bottom left, a weal is set up in the water; in the background, a man hanging at the gallows, several ships, and a city/port with a lighthouse; some wading birds near another weal in left middle ground; after Hans Bol
Engraving

Text line: "Sic agitat agandes ad l...oras..Salmones lolers medus viscator ab vndis."; numbered "34"
Translation: "thus the artful fisherman drives the large salmon from the open sea to the mouths of a river."

Design: Hans Bol (1534-1593)
Published in 1582 by Philips Galle (1537-1612)
Attributed to an anonymous engraver by New Hollstein (This one not catalogued with Adriaen Collaert).

Reference:
New Hollstein (Dutch & Flemish) / (undescribed)
Hollstein (110-163)

Good impression on laid paper. Full plate border and mostly tread margin.

Category: Art - Prints
