Title: The Martyrdom of S. John - Original

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617), after Maerten de Vos
Year: 1577 - 1582
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 187×270×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
In the right foreground is John, with a cup in his hand. This apostle died of natural causes, but endured some torture. They can be seen behind him: at the top left he is standing in a pan of boiling oil, in the middle plan he can be seen drinking a cup of poison. This print is part of a series of 13 prints of Christ (unnumbered) and the apostles (numbered), more specifically of the tortures they underwent. 
Series title: The Martyrdom of Christ and the Apostles 
print maker: Hendrick Goltzius 
after design by: Maerten de Vos 
publisher: weduwe Hieronymus Cock (mentioned on object)
