Title: Saint Peter tries to walk on water to Christ.

Artist: Philips Galle (1537-1612), Gerard van Groeningen (1550-1599)
Year: 1573
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 10×14.1×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Saint Peter tries to walk on water to Christ. 
Engraved by Philips Galle (1537-1612) ca 1573. 
He was a very good renaissance Flemish master engraver. He worked in Antwerp during the 80 years religious war. He worked for Hieronymus Cock, Engraved after Pieter Bruegel the Elder. 
He became a publisher in Antwerp and master for his sons and other engravers. 
After a drawing of Gerard van Groeningen (1550-1599). He was a Flemish renaissance painter, engraver and printdesigner. 
He worked in Antwerp. 
Published by Philips Galle (1537-1612) ca 1573 in Antwerp. 
From a rare series scenes of the new testament: "Christi Iesu vitae admirabiliumque actionum speculum a Philippo Gallaeo apparatum Bened. Ariae Montani singuloribus distichis instructum". 
New Hollstein Dutch 361-3(3) 
In perfect condition. Very good dark impression. 
Printed on good laid paper with large margins 1.5 cm x 3.2 cm. 
A fold in the middle nearly visible. 
Delivered in a museum passe-partout.
