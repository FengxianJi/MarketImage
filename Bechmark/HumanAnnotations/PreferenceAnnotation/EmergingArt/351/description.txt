Title: 11 views of Paris - Etchings of famous Paris churches

Artist: Jean Marot (1619-1679) - Stefano Della Bella (1610-1664)
Year: 1659
Technique: Engraving, Etching
Dimensions: 0×0×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Very rare engravings from the 1659 printed series “Recueil des plans, profils et élévations des plusieurs palais, chasteaux, églises...” 
Architecture is by Marot. The figures belong to the enigmatic works of Stefano della Bella. For a discussion of the co-operation of Marot and Stefano della Bella see: 
Kristina Deutsch, „« Marot. Il se nommait Jean… », Essai sur l’œuvre d’un graveur d’architecture du Grand Siècle, in Nouvelles des E`stampe, 236, 2011. 
Some prints are inscribed: "Dessignée et gravée par I. Marot" - "Avec privilege du Roy" - "Chez P. Mariette" 
Here, impressions with additional, separately printed ornamental frames (outstandingly rare!!!). 
Printed on high quality, laid paper. 
Size of view plate: ca. 13.5 x 26 cm; size of ornamental plate: ca. 19.6 x 31.7 cm; paper size: ca. 26 x 36 cm. 
Overall, in very good, almost mint condition. 
Very Rare !!!!
