Title: Synthese van Antwerpen

Artist: Joris Minne
Year: 1930
Technique: Woodcut print
Dimensions: 25.5×0×19 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Joris Minne, Atmosphere of Antwerp, 1930

Joris Minne (Ostend, 1897 - Antwerp, 1988) belonged to De Vijf (including Frans Masereel, Jan Frans & Jozef Cantré, and Henri Van Straten), the group of graphic artists who thoroughly modernized graphic art in Flanders in the 1920s. Minne then built up a long and stable career as a professor of visual arts.

These eight prints are part of the series "Atmosphere of Antwerp. Thirty-six wood engravings", published by the city council of Antwerp in 1930 with a foreword by Dr. Maurits Sabbe.

The 8 prints:
- 'Synthesis of Antwerp' including the Cathedral of Our Lady, the stepped gables from the old Vleehuisbuurt and industry;
- 'Tower with Scaffold': it is easy to recognize the KBC totes or 'Boerentoren'
- 'The stone'
- 'Masts'
- 'Tower with Shelves' - 'De Reede'
- 'Smoke'
- 'Blood Mountain'
- 'View of the Left Bank'
