Title: Curieuse aenmerckingen der bysonderste Oost en West-Indische dingen

Artist: Romeyn de Hooghe (1645-1708)
Year: 1682
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 1.95x1.5x0.01 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Rare Frontispiece etching by Romeyn de Hooghe: ‘Curieuse aenmerckingen der bysonderste Oost en West-Indische verwonderens-waerdige dingen. nevens die van China, Africa, en andere gewesten des werelds.’, published in Utrecht by J. Ribbius, 1682. 
The work contained numerous references to China. It was devoted to the natural and cultural world broadly defined, the Middle Kingdom and illustrates varied subjects .Etchings from this splendid work on the curiosities encountered during the ages of exploration abroad, are considered as of the most interesting of de Hooghe his oeuvre. 
Ref: Sabin, 19854. Tiele, 260. Cat. NHSM, 240. Muller, 1565.
