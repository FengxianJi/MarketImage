Title: Ostrich Hunt

Artist: Hans Bol (1534-1593), Adriaen Collaert (1560-1618)
Year: 1582
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 81×214×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Venationis, piscationis, et aucupii typi (Hunting, Fishing and Fowling Scenes) 
Men on horseback, wearing turbans, armed with shields and spears, surround an ostrich, while a man on foot holds a brace on the end of a pole against the bird's neck; dogs are involved in the pursuit; another ostrich is pursued in the background; palm trees at right; 
Desing by Hans Bol 
Print made by: Adriaen Collaert (attributed) 
Published by: Philips Galle 
"Strution insequitur celeri gens Maura molosso/Et Galeis plumas aptat gestamen honorum."; 
"The Moors pursue the ostrich with Molossian dogs, and fasten its feathers on their helmets as tokens of honour." 
Reference: 
New Hollstein  (1549) 
Hollstein  (110-163) 
A good impression with all detail.  Laid down on support sheet. 
Hans Bol was born in Mechelen. Prints after his own designs as well as Flemish masters such as Pieter Brueghel the Elder contributed to the spread of their themes in the Northern Netherlands. Master of Jacob Savery.
