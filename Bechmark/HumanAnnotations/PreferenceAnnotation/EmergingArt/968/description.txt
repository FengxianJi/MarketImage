Title: Tuinontwerp met parterres en loofgangen

Artist: Hans Vredeman De Vries (1527-1604), Dominicus Custos (c. 1559-1615)
Year: 1563
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 260×390×1 mm
Condition: N/A

Description:
Famous perspective print designed by Hans Vredeman De Vries (Leeuwarden 1527-1604) pioneering in terms of perspective and probably the most influential architectural designer ever.

Dimensions on plate edge: 190x 282mm

The print, from an undefined series of architectural prints, was probably made by Dominicus Custos (c. 1559-1615, his initials on one of the series after an earlier example by the Van Deuticum brothers. It is apparently a very rare work where no further information Not in the British Museum, not in the Rijksmuseum One found in the University Library of Salzburg, cataloged there as Hans Vredeman de Vries, circa 1580.

Rare!

Exceptionally good condition, excellent early printing for corner chamfering and with beautiful plate tone On handmade paper with early watermark.

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
