Title: Two etchings - Le Petit Port appelé aussi Le Port de Mer (The Small Port, also called the Sea)

Artist: Jacques Callot (1592-1635)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 22.0x8.3x0.0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Two etchings: 
1) "Le Petit Port appelé aussi Le Port de Mer "(The Small Port, also called the Sea Port). After 1618. 
Fine impression on laid paper. 
This charming etching depicts an harbour scene with ships on the left and people and buildings on the right. 
References: Bibliothèque Nationale II.199.267; Lieure I.103.267 ii/ii; Meaume 718. 
Condition report: Small restored loss at bottom left and small stains, otherwise good. 
2) Reverse copy of the etching "Le Moulin a Eau" (The Water Mill), plate 3 from the series "Les Quatre Paysages" (The Four Landscapes), also by Callot. 17th century. 
Very good impression on laid paper with watermak. 
Dimensions 8.5 x 21.5 cm. 
References: Reverse copy of Bibliothèque Nationale II.199.266; Lieure I.103.266 copy 2; Meaume 717. 
Condition report: Small stains and traces of mounting, otherwise very good.

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
