Title: Hottentots and habitants of Guinea-1682

Artist: Romeyn de Hooghe (1645-1708)
Year: 1682
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 2.0x1.5x0.01 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
A scarse etching from: ‘Curieuse aenmerckingen der bysonderste Oost en West-Indische verwonderens-waerdige dingen. nevens die van China, Africa, en andere gewesten des werelds.’, published in Utrecht by J. Ribbius, 1682. 
This etching from this splendid work on the curiosities encountered during the ages of exploration abroad is considered as part of some of the most interesting of his oeuvre. Ref: Sabin, 19854. Tiele, 260. Cat. NHSM, 240. Muller, 1565. 
In this scene different African tribes: Hottentots, Guineans, Inhabitants of Benin, Toupin Imbous, Caribes. In the background a triumpfant Karibes army and a watch tower. Rare.
