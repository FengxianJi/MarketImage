Title: Tempio di Giano

Artist: Giovanni Battista Piranesi (1720-1778), after, by Francis Vivares (1709-1780)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 16.0x29.0x0.01 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Good impression on wove paper. 
The temple of Janus, copy in reverse of the etching by Piranesi, plate 11 to the 'Antichità romane de'tempi della Repubblica, e de'primi imperatori' of 1748. 
Published by: Francis Vivares 
Curator's comments 
The original plate was published in 1748; this is plate 4 from a set of copies by Vivares, probably made ten or twenty years later.
