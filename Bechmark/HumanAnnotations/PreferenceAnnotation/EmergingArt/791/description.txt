Title: Bat - De Vespertilione

Artist: Conrad Gesner (1516-1565)
Year: 1555
Technique: Woodcut print
Dimensions: 37.5x23.5x0.1 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Beautiful hand colored woodcut of a bat from the FIRST EDITION of Conrad Gesner's "Icones animalium quadrupedum uiuiparorum et ouiparorum". 
From the first edition, being the latin one. 
Zürich, Christopher Froschauer, 1555. 
Dimensions: 37,5 x 23,5 cm. 
Reference: Nissen 350. Vischer C505(vol.3). 
Conrad Gesner is regarded the father of modern scientific bibliography, zoology and botany. 
He was a Renaissance polymath, physician, philologist, philosopher, natural historian, bibliographer and illustrator, spending most of his time collecting, researching and writing. He compiled monumental works on, amongst others, zoology (Historia Animalium 1551-1558). 
This beautiful colored woodcut was published in the 3 part of the "Historia Animaium", which is dedicated to birds. 
Very good condition.
