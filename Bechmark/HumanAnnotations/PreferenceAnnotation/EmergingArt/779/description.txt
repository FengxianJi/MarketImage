Title: Siege of Hulst, 1591 by the States army

Artist: Pieter Bast (Antwerp, 1550 - Leiden 1605)
Year: 1610
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 220×305×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Siege and capture of Hulst by Maurits, September 25, 1591. with surrounding land and water, at te bottom the fleet near the harbor.

By Pieter Bast (Antwerp, 1550 - Leiden 1605), attributed by Rijksmuseum
Dutch engraver and draftsman. Born in Antwerp, Child of Josyntje Plantijn, from the famous printer family Plantijn.

Town map engraved ca.1600-1601, published in J.J..Orlers: Den Nassauschen Lauren-Crans. Guaranteed impression from the 1615 edition.

Reference:
FMH 1005
Atlas van Stolk 932

Good early impression on laid paper with full plate border. Flattened middle fold. Excellent condition with no folds, tears or stains. Sheet 305 x 333 mm
