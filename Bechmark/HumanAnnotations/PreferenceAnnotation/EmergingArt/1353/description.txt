Title: t, Dronken Huÿsie t'enden 't bos - Haarlem

Artist: Jan Vincentsz. van der Vinne (1663-1721)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 15.3x18.4x0.01 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
t, Dronken Huÿsie t'enden 't bos 
(The drunken house at the end of the wood) 
This is one of the famous series: Gesigten buijten Haarlem (Views in the surroundings of Haarlem) made by Van de Vinne as part of a series of twelve around 1680-1700. 
A fine example of the realistic Dutch landscape from the Golden Age. 
View of a street in Haarlem; at left, a horse and cart, with the horses drinking from a trough 
Etching made by: Jan Vincentsz. van der Vinne 
Reference: 
Hollstein / Dutch and Flemish etchings, engravings and woodcuts c.1450-1700 (4.II) rdam (inv. 1904:41). 
Full plate border and margins, laid paper with letter watermark. 
Faded small stain in the sky, otherwise fine.
