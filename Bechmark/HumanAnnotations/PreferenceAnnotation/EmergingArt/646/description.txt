Title: A burning farmhouse with praying women

Artist: Abraham Bloemaert (1564-1651)
Year: 1650-1656
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 200×150×1 mm
Condition: In very good condition

Description:
This etching is from his famous 'drawing book'.  A series of etchings based on drawings by Abraham Bloemaert' . These helped to spread the powerful style of mannerism in the Netherlands and abroad. 
Abraham Bloemaert (1564 - 1651) 
Genre, portrait and history painter, etcher and engraver; b. at Dordrecht, d. in Utrecht; son of Cornelis Bloemaert; pupil of Joost de Beer in Utrecht; 1580-83 pupil of H. Francken in Paris; 1591-92 in Amsterdam. 
Etched by his son Frederik Bloemaert (1614-1690) 
Good impression on watermarked laid paper. 
Full plate border and tread margins.
