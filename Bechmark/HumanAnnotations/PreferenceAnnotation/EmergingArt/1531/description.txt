Title: Galerie Louis Carré (after), Fernand Leger, 1953

Artist: Fernand Léger (1881-1955) 
Year: N/A
Technique: Lithograph
Dimensions: 0×470×650 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
An extremely rare lithographic poster for an exhibition of work by Fernand Leger (1881-1955) held in June 1953 at Louis Carré Gallery 
Printed in 1953 and was supervised by Fernand Léger himself at the Mourlot's studios. Moreover, this particular lithograph has a on the bottom right corner indicating that it was probably displayed in France, hence, making it even more sought after. 
Good overall condition, creasing, tears and paper losses (see images)
