Title: Lot of 3 engravings Ornithology, Birds of Prey: Osprey, Hen Harrier, Goshawk. Hand coloured by

Artist: Edward Donovan (1768-1837)
Year: 1794
Technique: Hand coloured by Donovan himself in 1794!, Etching, Watercolour
Dimensions: 22.8x13.3x0.1 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
3 engravings with original handcoloring by Donovan himself!! 
An exquisite bird print, engraved and hand coloured by the artist. 
Nissen IVB 257; Mullens & Swann, p 173. 
'The hand-coloring of these birds was delicately and beautifully done, giving the book considerable artistic merit. He painted the figures smoothly with gouache.' (Jackson, C. (1985) Bird Etchings, p. 183).
