Alberto Giacometti (1901-1966) 
Nu debout, 1961 
This is an original lithograph, crafted with Giacomettis renowned sculptural sensibilities. Executed in nuanced lines, it evokes a sense of existential contemplation through the solitary, elongated figure, emblematic of Giacometti's iconic style. The lithograph encapsulates Giacometti's ability to convey both physical presence and psychological depth in his art.
