Title: De grote Ecce Homo

Artist: Naar Lucas van Leyden (1494-1533)
Year: 1600
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 32.2×49.4×0.1 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
The great Ecce Homo: Christ shown to the people by Pilate, standing on a raised square in front of Pilate's palace. Many bystanders, some furiously looking at Christ. 
Large format etching by an unknown master, after Lucas van Leyden (1494-1533). Undated. Manufactured ca. 1600. 
Extremely rare. 
Catalog references: 
- New Hollstein 71, copy 
- Bartsch 71, copy 
See also copy from the Metropolitan Museum in New York, Accession Number: 47.100.94. 
Good condition. Short margins. Paper slightly soiled and slightly stained. Verso: blank. Attached in the corners at the top to a later bottom sheet. 
Image dimensions: 27.8 x 44.3 cm.
