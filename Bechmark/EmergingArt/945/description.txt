Title: chromointerference Manipulable

Artist: Carlos Cruz-Diez (1923-2019)
Year: 2009
Technique: Mixed media
Dimensions: 25×25×3 cm
Condition: N/A

Description:
Framed, lenticular little card by Carlos Cruz-Diez. 
The work is called a Chromointerference.  It is from 2009. 
The card is made with a lenticular effect, you can see the effect of the work changing as you look from a different angle. 
There are 3 different lenticular effects - showing different colored circles combinations. 
Dimensions work 14cm x 14cm. 
Dimensions frame 25cm x 25cm x 4cm. 
These are very hard to find on the market, as it was issued in very limited numbers. 
The work is in very good condition.
