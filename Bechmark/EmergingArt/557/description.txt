Condition: In very good condition. 
The Indian ink drawing was designed between 1940 and 1943 by Otto Freundlich before being enlarged on a 1,4 scale and screen printed. 
Printed by Atelier Fanal and Max Fürer, Bâle. 
From "Hommage à Otto Freundlich" portfolio,  published by "Les Amis de Jeanne et Otto Freundlich" in 1989.
