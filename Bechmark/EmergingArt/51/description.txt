NICOLAS BAZIN (1633-1710) AFT RAPHAEL 
Selling here is an original 17th century engraving by Nicolas Bazin 1633-1710 AFT Raphael. With good sized margins and a decent plate definition. 
The engraving reproduces a composition resembling that of the Saint Margaret, a painting currently in the Musée du Louvre, Paris (inv. no.607), the execution of which is generally ascribed to Giulio Romano after designs by Raphael (c.1518). The print differs from the original painting in a few details, including the cross that the saint hold wih her left hand.
