Marc Chagall (1887-1985) 
Esquisse pour L’Ange rouge, 1965 
Printer's proof original lithograph on wove paper, from Marc Chagall's design, "L’Ange rouge". 
Blind stamp "RGM" lower right margin. 
Dimensions: 
+ Size of the sheet: 33 x 29 cm / 12,9 x 11,4 in. 
Condition: In very good condition. May have some soft handling creases at the edges. 
This design was used for the 1965 portfolio "Chagall. Le Plafond de L'Opera de Paris". 
This Art book was published by André Sauret to celebrate the completion of the Paris Opera's ceiling with Marc Chagall's paintings in 1964. The portfolio displayed several trial proofs leading to the final designs. 
Lithographed by Charles Sorlier. 
Printed by Imp. Mourlot, Paris. 
Provenance: Robert Gustave Michel (RGM), Michel Gallery.
