Title: Fountain of the four rivers - Piazza Navona - Bernini

Artist: Joan Blaeu (c. 1599-1673) 
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 5.6x3.5x0.01 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
The fountain of the four rivers in the Piazza Navona, Rome, designed by Giovanni Lorenzo Bernini (1598-1680), as a base for the obelisk removed from before the Baths of Caracalla. 
This engraving was originally published by Joan Blaeu. By the year 1672 a fire destroyed the main Blaeu workshop and much of the prepared material. In 1682 the work was re-published in Amsterdam by his heirs. A later publication was issued by Pieter Mortier in 1604. 
Good early impression on thick dense laid paper with full plate border and margins. Excellent condition with no folds, tears or stains. 
Plate border: 56 x 35 cm
