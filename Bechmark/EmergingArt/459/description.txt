Title: Recumbent sheep 

Artist: After Karel Dujardin (c.1626-1678)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 0.97x1.38x0.01 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Landscape with a recumbent sheep , in three-quarter view and his head facing left right in profile, its coat partially shed, Copy after Karel Dujardin of the etching generally known as The sheep and the flies, but in this copy without the fies.

Karel Dujardin (1626-1678) 
Dutch Romanist painter and etcher, best known for his spirited representations of Italian peasants and shepherds with their animals.Dujardin was a son of the painter Guilliam Dujardin. After a trip to Italy, he worked in Amsterdam and The Hague from 1652 until 1674; after that he returned to Rome, where he stayed until shortly before his death. 
A very good sharp impression with embossed plate borders on thin vellum paper,  in good condition.
