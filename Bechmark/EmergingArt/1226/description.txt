Title: Zittend meisje met mand op den rug

Artist: Jozef Israels (1824-1911)
Year: 1879
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 270×390×0 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
Fairly rare etching, see also the photo of the description in Hubert's book.
Literature: Catalog Hubert no. XIX.
Loose-leaf. Image size 142x219 mm.
Jozef Israëls was born in Groningen on January 27, 1824 and died in The Hague on August 12, 1911.
Trained at the Minerva Academy in Groningen, the Royal Academy of Visual Arts in Amsterdam and the Ecole des Beaux Arts in Paris.
Teacher of his son Isaac, D.A.C.Artz and B.J.Blommers.
Belonged to the Hague School.
Member of Arti et Amicitiae, Pulchri Studio and the Royal Academy of Arts, London.
