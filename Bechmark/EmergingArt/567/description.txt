Title: The Martyrdom of Saint Matthew

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617)
Year: 1577 - 1582
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 186×272×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Matthew is beaten to death with a sword while reading wrong; to the left of the scene of his martyrdom he is depicted again, with a carpenter's square in his hand. Below the performance twice two lines of Latin. This print is part of a series of 13 prints of Christ (unnumbered) and the apostles (numbered), more specifically of the tortures they underwent. 
Series title: The Martyrdom of Christ and the Apostles 
print maker: Hendrick Goltzius 
after design by: Maerten de Vos 
publisher: weduwe Hieronymus Cock (mentioned on object)

The Illustrated Bartsch 295-d 
Hollstein Dutch 358
