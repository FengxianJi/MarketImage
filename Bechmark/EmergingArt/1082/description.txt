About the artist:

Salvatore Fiume was an Italian painter. He was also a sculptor, architect, writer and set designer.
After training at the Royal Art Institute of the Book of Urbino, he moved to Milan where he established relationships with important intellectuals of the time, including Quasimodo and Buzzati.

Salvatore Fiume's mature works are strongly influenced by Gauguin's art and exotic atmospheres: warm tones, strong color contrasts, simple shapes, fantastic themes and almost metaphysical settings. The protagonists of this creative period are curvy and sensual Mediterranean and oriental women. Today Salvatore Fiume's works are preserved in important private collections and Italian and foreign museums, including the Vatican Museums, the Hermitage in St. Petersburg, the MoMA in New York, the Pushkin Museum in Moscow.
