Title: The last judgement

Artist: Dirck Barendsz. (1534-1592)
Year: 1575
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 230×310×1 mm
Condition: In good condition

Description:
The Four Last Things

Last Judgement. Jesus Christ seated on a cloud at top centre surrounded by Apostles, an angel leading the saved souls to Heaven at left and a demon leading the damned souls to Hell at right; the dead rising from their graves in foreground; after Dirk Barendsz
Engraving

Lettered in Latin in lower margin, two lines in two columns: "Christe parentis amor, ... / ... ipse forum.".

Measurements on plate border: 188 x 250 mm

Fascinating engraving in an excellent early impression with good detail and nice plate tone, plate borders still smuggled as to be found in early impression. A closed hole on the head of the bottom left person, paper inset and redrawn. Very good general appearance.

This print is after the Sadeler print with the same subject.

Reference:
Hollstein / Dutch and Flemish etchings, engravings and woodcuts c.1450-1700 (452), after.
