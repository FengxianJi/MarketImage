Title: Tribe of Reuben ‘Jerusalem Windows’ 
Author: Marc Chagall 
Medium: Colour lithograph (after gouache) 
Issued: Andre Sauret - Monte Carlo 1962 
Printed: Mourlot, France 1962 
Issued: George Braziller, Inc. New York. 
Dimensions: 31.5 x 24.5 cm 
Each of the twelve stained-glass windows was reproduced and provided with a preparatory set of drawings and models. There, the number, media, dimensions, and order of the presentation are exactly the same in the order of each tribe and arranged as follows: 
First sketch 
Pre-drawing 
First colour sketch 
Small model 
Final model 
The colour designs were designed by Charles Sorlier and printed under the direction of March Chagall onto the stonesand printed by Mourlot. The other paintings and reproductions are by Draeger Fréres. 
The lot consists of four sheets, two of which are two-sided.
