Title: You never know when death is knocking on your door

Artist: Theodore Galle (1571-1633)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 160×85×1 mm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Hoc age, quod in morte egisse velis 
[Tue das, was Du im Tod vollbracht haben willst] 
Do now what you want to have accomplished before death 
You never know who's knocking on your door, death can strike anytime. 
Do a pelgrimage before it is too late. 
Engraving from the first edition of Jan David (ca.1545–1613). Veridicus Christianus and Christeliicken waerseggher., issued in Antwerp by Jan Moretus at the Plantin Press in 1603. 
A collection of emblems dealing with sins of mankind and the fear for death and hell. 
Explanation of the scene in three languages beneath print. 
No text on backside, no letterpress, all text engraved in the plate. 
Full sheet with margins, soiled margins and a closed tear from left to right through the middle. 
On plate border: 160 x 86 mm. Good early impression from the first edition. 
Very inspired emblems from the contra reformation period in early baroque style! 
Theodor Galle (1571-1633) was the creator/engraver perhaps in cooperation with his brother Cornelis Galle (1576-1650) 
Theodoor Galle ( 1571 - 1633) 
Engraver and publisher from Antwerp. Son of Philips, took over workshop on his death in 1612. Travelled with his brother, Cornelis I, to Italy in 1596. Married daughter of Antwerp publisher Jan Moretus. Introduced collaboration between Galle workshop and Plantin Press.
