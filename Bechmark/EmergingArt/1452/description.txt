Title: An allegory of painting, with Leonardo da Vinci. Large engraving: 'Tanto che basti'.

Artist: Carlo Maratti (1625-1713), Nicolas Dorigny (1658-1746).
Year: 1704
Technique: Engraving, Etching
Dimensions: 40.8×31.8×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
An allegory of painting, with Leonardo da Vinci. Large engraving: 'Tanto che basti'.
'As long as it is enough'

Leonardo da Vinci points at a statue of écorché and the antique statues of Hercules, Venus, Callipygon and Apollo. Three times the text 'Tanto che basti' engraved. In front a painter (Maratti) explaining perspective calculations. (source Bellori's biography of Maratta).

Engraving/etching by Nicolas Dorigny (1658-1746) ca 1704-1710. He was a famous French Baroque engraver, who worked during the reign of King Louis XIV.

After a drawing made for the Marchese del Carpio by Carlo Maratti (1625-1713). He was an Italian Late Baroque painter, a classicizing paintings.

Engraving published in Rome ca 1704-1710.

Engraving/etching in good condition, missing the text border below. with a watermark : french lily in two circles. On laid paper with 2 mm blank paper around the image, on the margins, image is complete.
A good dark impression of this rare engraving/etching.

References: IFF / Inventaire du Fonds Français: Bibliothèque Nationale, Département des Estampes (97)
