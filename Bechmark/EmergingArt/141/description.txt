Title: Brokenbrow etchings (6)

Artist: Georg Grosz (1893-1959), after
Year: 1923
Technique: Photogravure
Dimensions: 220×160×0 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
A complete set of 6 etchings by Georg Grosz to illustrate 'Brokenbrow' by Ernst Toller. Printed on cream laid paper sheets by the Curwen Press for the Nonesuch Press in 1923. The only contemporary publication of these etchings. 
All in good condition on the full sheets c.230 x 180 mm. reverse blank.
