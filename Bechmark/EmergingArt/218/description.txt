Title: Christ and Nicodemus - Very rare engraving

Artist: Joan Wierix (1539-1620), after
Year: 1573
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 1.6x1.85x0.01 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Engraving attributed to Johannes Wierix (1539-1620) 
This is from  the very rare work : Christi Jesu vitae admirabiliumque actionum speculum. 
First issued in Antwerp in  1573. Only a dozen of copies known.(18?) 
According to Mauquoy-hendrickx, the designs are by  Gerard van Groeningen (1550-1599) The engraver Joan Wierix  signed some of the plates. Most of the unsigned plates are also attributed to him, others to the workshop of Philips Galle. (1537-1612) 
(Les estampes de Wierix, III.1 page 395-396) 
The engraving is from an edition by Joan Galle (1600-1676) and a state before removal of the Latin verses on the print in the last edition for Visscher in 1642. 
The prints are rare, hard to find anywhere, only a dozen copies known. Not in British museum, not in the Rijksmuseum Amsterdam.  Some bound with Divinarum nuptiarum conventa et acta'. See BM 2AA+,a.20.1 and 2AA+,a.20.2. 
Condition: Good impression in a sheet of laid paper with good margins. Tear in margin. 
Last image is taken on the lightbox. 
Measurements: 92x137 mm on plate border.
