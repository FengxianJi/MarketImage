Description:
Christo & Jeanne Claude: 
"The Mastaba", a project for Abu Dhabi, conceived in 1977 by Christo and Jeanne-Claude and pending execution. 
2 ARTCARDs (published by Taschen and Schumacher Edition). Mounted on Rembrandt quality paper 160 gr. 
The montage includes 5 fabric fragments from other famous installations by Christo and Jeanne Claude: The Umbrellas USA (1991), Wrapped Trees (1998), Floating Piers (2016), Running Fence (1976) and Wrapped Walk Ways (1978). They are not samples, but original fabrics from the projects.. 
Framed (deep-box) with glass. Size: 31 x 22.5 cm.
