Title: Paris, la Seine aux quatre ponts

Artist: D'après Robert Delaunay (1885-1941)
Year: N/A
Technique: Lithograph
Dimensions: 28×22.5 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Comment of the appraiser: In 1907, Robert Delaunay began his artistic work on the monuments of Paris, which he decided to continue in 1926 with the production of some twenty lithographs, including Paris, La Seine and the Tower. The influence of Cubism, and in particular Picasso, on the artist can be felt immediately in this lithograph. This lithograph is a characteristic work of the period when Delaunay questioned the relevance of cubism and futurism before moving on to abstraction with Orphism and Simultanism. To make the link and understand the work in more detail, we advise you to read the biography of the artist below.
