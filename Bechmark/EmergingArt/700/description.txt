Description:
Village view of [presumably] Rijnsburg. Gray wash pencil drawing, attributed to Johannes Huibert Prins (1757-1806). Manufactured ca. 1780-1790. 
Good condition. Light spotting. Verso: blank. Mounted on an 18th century base sheet. 
Drawing dimensions: 10.0 x 15.4 cm. 
Bottom sheet dimensions: 12.2 x 17.5 cm. 
For comparison see, for example, the other drawings by Prins in the collection of the Rijksmuseum Amsterdam, and in particular his drawing of the ruin at Rijnsburg from 1781 (object number RP-T-1905-128). 
Johannes Huibert Prins (The Hague, 1757 – Utrecht, 1806) was a well-known Dutch painter and watercolorist of landscapes and cityscapes. He was self-taught. His work can be found in the collections of the Rijksmuseum Amsterdam, Teylers Stichting in Haarlem and the Museum Boymans-van Beuningen Rotterdam, among others.
