Title: Amphitheatre Colosseum, Rome, Italy 1624

Artist: Giacomo Lauro (1550–1605), also called Giacomo da Treviso
Year: 1624
Technique: Engraving, Watercolour
Dimensions: 21.8×30.7×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Giacomo Lauro (1550–1605), also called Giacomo da Treviso, was an Italian painter, of the late Renaissance, active mainly in his native Venice and Treviso. He was a follower of Paolo Veronese. He painted a St. Roch interceding for victims of the plague. He died in Treviso 
The Arch of Constantine and its Ancient Surrounds. The Arch of Constantine was located between the Colosseum and the Palatine Hill.
