Title: "Martirio di Santa Cecilia"da Raffaello Sanzio.

Artist: Marcantonio Raimondi (c.1480-1527/34)
Year: 1650
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 235×0×405 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Saint Cecilia standing naked in a large cauldron, in a gesture of prayer she raises her eyes to the angel above holding the crown of a martyr; two figures support the severed heads of the saint's husband and brother-in-law; on the left, under a colonnade, sits a Roman Prefect, on the right the statue of Jupiter

after a preparatory drawing by Raphael for the fresco of the Martyrdom of Santa Cecilia in the Chapel of San Giovanni in Villa Papale, commissioned by Leo X and executed by the School of Raphael between 1517-1919.

Category: Art - Prints
Signature: Not signed
