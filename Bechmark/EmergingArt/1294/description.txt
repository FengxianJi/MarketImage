Title: Rencontre de Rachel et Jacob (The meeting of Rachel and Jacob)

Artist: Marc Chagall (1887-1985)
Year: 1939
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 44×33.6×0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
The Meeting of Rachel and Jacob was etched by Chagall sometime between 1931 and 1939 for Vollard's planned publication of a large  portfolio with 105 original etchings illustrating the Bible. The size of the composition is  29,5 x 24,5 cm.  Due to the war, Chagalls exile in the US and Vollards death in 1939  the edition was not published until 1956 when Teriade - who had bought the  plates from Vollards estate -published the portfolio. The 105 etchings were printed in two editions:  295 copies in black/and white, unsigned and unnumbered on sheets 44 x 33,6 cm and 100 copies on slightly larger sheets numbered and signed with the initials. 
This copy has two remains of mounting verso upper margin but no discolouration, no marks of a previous framing
