Title: Arrest of Christ in the garden of Gethsemani  - Rare first state before the number

Artist: Jacques Callot (1592-1632)
Year: Ca. 1624-1625
Technique: Etching
Dimensions: 77×59×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Arrest of Christ in the garden of Gethsemani, with Judas kissing him, in the foreground, St Peter attacking a soldier with a sword. c.1624/25 
Etching 
Rare first state before the number. 
Lieure calls for fine first states only to fully enjoy the finesse of this work. 
Described by Lieure as 'Extremely fine and spiritual' 
by Mariette as '...some of the most beautiful things that J. Callot invented and engraved since his return from Italy...' 
Etcher: Jacques Callot 
Reference: 
Lieure 1927 / Jacques Callot (540. I / II ) 
Meaume 1860 / Recherches sur la vie et les ouvrages de Jacques Callot (22.I) 
From a series of twelve etchings representing the Passion of Christ; the set was probably designed and engraved after Callot's return in Nancy, c.1624/25. A terminus ante quem is provided by a contract between Callot and Henriet dated 11 April 1631 about a series concerning the life of the Virgin, stating that the said series will have to be of the same quality as 'la petite Passion du Christ'. 
With plate border and tread margins, on plate border near top right corner. Tipped with corners on a collection sheet. A good detailed impression. 
Jacques Callot ( 1592 - 1635) 
Etcher and draughtsman, born Nancy. 1608-1611 in Rome; 1612-1621 in Florence, working for the Medici; in 1621 returned to Nancy, Died suddenly.
