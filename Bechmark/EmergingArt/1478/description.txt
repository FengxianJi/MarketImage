Title: Hortus Eystettensis - Wild Delphinium

Artist: Basilius Besler (1561 - 1629)
Year: 1613
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 55×43×0.1 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Original antique copper engraving from the monumental botanical folio Hortus Eystettensis, published in Germany by Basilius Besler in 1613.
This engraving is plate 159 showing wild Delphinium or Wolfsbane and is printed on hand laid paper, there is beautiful Latin text to the verso of the print.

The overall condition is very good, with great colour and detail, there is a light crease across the centre and a small stain between the Latin text at the bottom.
The piece measures 55cm X 43cm.
The artwork is supplied with a beautiful marbled mount.
