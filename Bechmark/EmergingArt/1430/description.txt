Title: The Siege of Zaltbommel

Artist: Bartholomeus Willemsz. Dolendo (1571-1627)
Year: N/A
Technique: Etching

Description:
Siege by the Spaniards under Mendoza of Zaltbommel and other strongholds in the Bommelerwaard in the hands of the State army under Prince Maurits, and the construction of the Sint-Andries redoubt, early May to September 1599. On the left the Maas, below the Waal, in the middle Bommel. 
By Bartholomeus  Dolendo (1571-1627), (Attribution by the Rijsmuseum) 
Engraved ca.1601-1610, 
Published in J.J..Orlers: Den Nassauschen Lauren-Crans.
