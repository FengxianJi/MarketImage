Pieter Holsteyn II (Haarlem ca. 1614 – Amsterdam 1673) was a Dutch painter, draftsman and engraver. He worked in Haarlem, Zwolle, Enkhuizen, Münster and Amsterdam. Holsteyn was best known for his engraved portraits and watercolors of birds and flowers. 
Isabella d'Este (Ferrara, 18 May 1474 - Ferrara, 13 February 1539) was Marchioness of Mantua and one of the leading figures of the Italian Renaissance. She also had great political influence. 
At the age of sixteen she married Francesco II Gonzaga, the Marquis of Mantua. She went to live in Mantua. Under her supervision, this court became one of the most civilized in Europe. She started collecting Roman sculptures and also commissioned new sculptures in classical style. Musicians, artists, writers and thinkers flocked to Mantua in large numbers, including Raphael and Leonardo da Vinci. She herself was a gifted musician and had special music made as a backdrop to poems. 
When her husband was captured in 1509 and held hostage in Venice, she took command of the army and defended Mantua until his return in 1512. On his return, he felt jealousy for his wife, whose policies had been more successful than his. Isabella corresponded with a large number of people, including relatives and scholars. Her great development is reflected in her letters.
