Description:
CHRISTO & JEANNE CLAUDE. 
Christo Javacheff (1935-2020) & Jeanne-Claude Denat de Guillebon (1935-2009). 
Surrounded Islands (1983) / The Gates (2005) 
ONE giant banners with representation back and forth  for the exhibition "Museum Würth Collection. Completed Large-Scale Public Art Projects", at the Bass Museum of Art in Miami Beach. Held in 2005 and in the location of one of the most impressive installations of the Land-Art couple (Surrounded Islands). 
The exhibition brought together more than 65 collages, drawings, photographs and scale models of Christo and Jeanne-Claude's most important projects. 
The banner measures 240 x 90 cm, made of thick canvas synthetic fabric and printed on BOTH SIDES (one with the motif of a preparatory drawing for the installation that same year, The Gates, in New York. The other with a motif of Sourronded Islands, Miami). 
It is in perfect condition, complete with rings and loops to hang it. With semicircular openings to offer resistance to the wind and avoid tears.
