Title: The Martyrdom of Bartholomew

Artist: Hendrick Goltzius (1558-1617)
Year: 1577 - 1582
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 186×270×1 mm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Bartholomew is skinned by Indians. Some of the depicted Indians wear a feather headdress and therefore appear to be depicted as the original inhabitants of America. To the left of the scene of his martyrdom he is depicted again, with a knife in his hand.  This print is part of a series of thirteen prints of Christ (unnumbered) and the apostles (numbered), more specifically of the tortures they underwent. 
Series title: The Martyrdom of Christ and the Apostles 
print maker: Hendrick Goltzius 
after design by: Maerten de Vos 
publisher: weduwe Hieronymus Cock (mentioned on object) 
Reference: 
The Illustrated Bartsch 295-c 
Hollstein Dutch 357
