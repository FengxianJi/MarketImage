Title: Adoration of the shepherds - Birth of Christ 

Artist: Nicolaes Jansz Clock (1576 -1602)
Year: 1582
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 17.9×11.8×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
Adoration of the shepherds. Birth of Christ. Very rare engraving. 
Engraving made by Nicolaes Jansz. Clock (1576 -1602) after his own drawing. 
Printed and published by him in 1582. 
He was a Dutch painter and engraver working in Leyden and Haarlem. 
He was a pupil of Hendrick Goltzius. 
There are only a few engravings made by Nicolaes Jansz Clock. 
Most of them are not religious. 
This is a rare engraving of him. 
Only in a few museums. 
References: Hollstein Dutch 2. 
Very early and very dark impression, in excellent condition. 
With the ruling lines to engrave the text clearly visible only with the first impressions. 
Printed on thin laid paper, on the margins. 
Delivered in a museum passe-partout.
