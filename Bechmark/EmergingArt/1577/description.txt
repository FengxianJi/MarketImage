A litograph from the Picasso's sketchbook on bull fighting. Second edition from 1962.

These drawings by Picasso covered a period from 1950 to 1960, during which time he enjoyed seeing Dominguin bullfight in the arenas of Nîmes, Arles, Frejus and Vallauris.

The leading copies of this publication contain a series of reproductions of color drawings selected from the first two notebooks, as well as a lithograph of February 7, 1961. (Wikipedia).

Very good condition.


"With tears streaming down her face, the woman in this Pablo Picasso print looks back at the viewer from behind a veil capped with a crown.

The highly-emotional piece may refer to the Virgin Mary as she weeps for the injured and dead bullfighters." - RoGallery
