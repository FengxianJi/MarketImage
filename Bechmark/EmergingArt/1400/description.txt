Phototype was a 125-year-old form of lithography that has now been discontinued, in which an almost photographic reproduction was achieved with a maximum of fourteen colour printing passes (with special, decades-long lightfast colours), this by far the finest printing technique is also no longer feasible today. Not even with the most modern production techniques.

Due to the time-consuming, manual and therefore cost-intensive production process, the small number of artist or museum commissions and the lack of future prospects for the specialised professionals, this most faithful of all known printing processes is considered "extinct" and has not been exported globally for two decades.

That is why these last classic phototypes are something special and irreparable for any lover of historical printing.
