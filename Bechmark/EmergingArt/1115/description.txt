Title: Perspective view of a square with town hall and round church - Early state

Artist: Hans Vredeman De Vries ( 1527-1604)
Year: N/A
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 260×390×1 mm
Condition: In very good condition

Description:
Perspective print designed by Hans Vredeman De Vries (Leeuwarden 1527-1604) pioneering in terms of perspective and probably the most influential architectural designer ever.

Dimensions on plate edge: 178 x 275 mm

From an architectural design not further specified with streets, squares, courtyards and rooms.

Reference for equivalent work:: Floral prints from the 15th and 16th century in the Rijks Museum in Amsterdam, page 98 and following

The print here was probably made by Dominicus Custos (c. 1559-1615), his initials on one of the series. It is apparently a very rare work of which no further information can be found. Not in the British Museum, not in the Rijksmuseum. One found in the University Library of Salzburg, cataloged there as Hans Vredeman de Vries, circa 1580.
Rare!

Exceptionally good condition, excellent pressure apparently for chamfering the corners and with beautiful plate tone.
