I am selling a beautiful small sculpture form Julio Le Parc. 
This sculpture uses a mirror and a printed side inside, and is made of plexiglass. By looking into the sculpture from a different angle you will see the circles changing color. 
This work was designed in 1965, and produced in 2015. 
Dimensions of the piece 95mm x 80mm x 67mm.
