Title: "September"

Artist: Etienne Delaune (1518-1583) 
Year: 1570
Technique: Burin
Dimensions: 1.81x2.45x0.0 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Small and rare engraving depicting Venus crowning a young satyr at her side, perhaps part of a larger engraving, monogram "S" at the bottom that refers to the signature of the French goldsmith and medallist Etienne Delaune, one of the most famous of his time, an artist who also engraved numerous prints, generally small, executed entirely with burin and usually signed S., S. F., S. fecit or Stephanus, fecit. 
Copy in good condition (see photos).
