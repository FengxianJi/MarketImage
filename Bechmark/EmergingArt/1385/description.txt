Artist: Eduard Winkler (1799-1862)
Year: 1792
Technique: Engraving, Watercolour
Dimensions: 24×16×0 cm
Condition: Fair condition

Description:
Eduard Winkler (1799-1862), a German botanist, scholar and teacher.

These prints are from a beautiful work encompassing a comprehensive study of flora & a few fauna with pharmaceutical properties. It represented a botanical study for medicine and pharmacy as well as homeopathic practitioners.

The prints are on white paper, with each subject beautifully arranged on the page, showing fruit, flowers, leaves and other segments. They are good impressions, with beautiful, original hand-coloring.
The condition of these prints is very good There is few of no foxing.
All our prints are authentic hand colored antique prints, and the Winkler's are more that 160 years old in remarkable condition.
