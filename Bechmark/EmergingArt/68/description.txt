Title: The hunt on monkeys.

Artist: Hans Bol (1534-1593), Philips Galle (XVI)
Year: 1582-1633
Technique: Engraving
Dimensions: 7.8×22×0 cm
Condition: Good condition

Description:
The hunt on monkeys. A renaissance style huntingscene. 
The hunters on foot and on horses shoot with arrows at the monkeys. 
Engraving after Hans Bol (1534-1593). Hans Bol was one of the leading Flemish renaissance painters. His drawings and paintings were designs for prints published by Hieronymus Cock, Philips Galle and others. 
Engravings after his landscapes were very admired in the 16th and 17th century. 
Engraved by one of the best engravers Philips Galle (1537-1612) in 1582. 
One of a serie of 54 hunting and fish scenes, after the drawings of Hans Bol. 
Hans Bol was a collegue painter of Pieter Bruegel the Elder and Maerten de Vos. 
Published by Theodore Galle in Antwerp in 1582-1633. 
Number 14. 
These engravings are considered as one of the most beautiful hunting and fishing engravings after Hans Bol.
