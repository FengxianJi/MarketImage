Original etching by Pierre-Auguste Renoir (1841 - 1919).
Title: baigneuse debout à mi-jambes (1910). Unsigned.

Printed on cream coloured vergé.

A few tiny creases in blank margins. Two minimal foxing spots. Overall good condition.
