Title: Limited edition Original serigraph

Artist: Charles Hossein Zenderoudi
Year: 1972
Technique: Silkscreen / Serigraph
Dimensions: 20×26×0.1 cm
Condition: Excellent condition

Description:
Offered here is an authentic and limited edition screenprint by the acclaimed artist Charles Hossein Zenderoudi. 
It is part of a series of illustrations created for the edition of the Le Coran by Lebaud editions in 1972. 
This particular piece measures 20cm x 26cm and is part of a limited edition, with only 373 copies ever produced. 
According to the text in the last photo (The page from Quran), the provided work is one of Hossein Zenderoud's 12 original serigraphs printed on Vélin de Rives and the work provided here is from exemplaire  No. 97.
